# Testing Day Feedback
## General GUI Feedback
In general, after using the GUI, some users could use the GUI well, while others had trouble figuring out how to start capturing packets. Most testers commented that the start, stop and pause buttons were too small, which was an unexpected bug that occurred only on the testing device.

![image](https://github.com/spe-uob/2023-NetworkTrafficAnalysis/assets/123552121/8677dae0-eb46-4ddd-99e7-6a3ce6a65bbd)

### <img align="right" src="https://github.com/spe-uob/2023-NetworkTrafficAnalysis/assets/123552121/a775f165-161e-4513-aa4a-e9a75425728b"> Consistency
All testers disagreed or strongly disagreed that the GUI elements were inconsistent.

This tells us that the testers could not find any major inconsistencies within the GUI elements, making improving consistency unneccessary.

### <img align="left" src="https://github.com/spe-uob/2023-NetworkTrafficAnalysis/assets/123552121/9b6c3db3-fdf0-45c6-a4a1-e4469a4c45ff"> Noise
After testing, testers were asked whether they thought the GUI was too cluttered. These responses were quite mixed, but the majority disagreed with the statement.

There is still room for decluttering within the GUI, such as getting rid of some less used windows, but overall the GUI isn't too cluttered.

### <img align="right" src="https://github.com/spe-uob/2023-NetworkTrafficAnalysis/assets/123552121/17f40bdc-04ce-4f5a-9ce4-a199b47cf24c"> Intuitiveness
The intuitiveness of the GUI was judged by asking testers whether or not they found using the GUI intuitive. Results ranged from mixed to strong agreement.

Overall, while results tended to be positive, not all testers found the GUI intuitive. This can be improved by adding instructions to support lost users.

### <img align="left" src="https://github.com/spe-uob/2023-NetworkTrafficAnalysis/assets/123552121/0147ec21-c963-4952-b65b-3839d2b47676"> Usefulness
Testers were asked how much they agree with the statement "A Network Engineer would find the packet information provided useful". All testers agreed or strongly agreed.

This is expected, as the program provides a lot of packet information. Some of these details could be considered unnecessary, such as the alternate representation of packet byte data.

It is not ideal to test this kind of question on inexperienced network analysts, and testing this on network engineers in the future is vital.

###  <img align="right" src="https://github.com/spe-uob/2023-NetworkTrafficAnalysis/assets/123552121/98a23583-2605-4d76-909e-041b4ace4585"> Visual Style
After testing the new GUI, testers were shown a screenshot of the old GUI and asked to compare the appropriateness of the visual style. Responses were very mixed.

Some comments against the changed GUI include comparisons to Wireshark, a packet capturing tool with a similar visual style to last year's GUI. Others thought that the new purple and black colour scheme doesn't provide enough contrast to easily make out important buttons.

## Attack Analysis Window Feedback
Response to the attack analysis window was generally positive. Most users found navigating between analyses long and cumbersome; a good improvement would be to replace the multiple links to click on with a side tab allowing quick switching between analyses. Opinions were mixed as to whether to keep the attack analysis window as a seperate tab, or to integrate it into the GUI elements.

### <img align="left" src="https://github.com/spe-uob/2023-NetworkTrafficAnalysis/assets/123552121/e8d87862-de0a-4a92-bede-91363ded363d"> Efficiency
Responses to the statement "The attack analyses take too long" were mixed, but tended towards disagreement.

This tells us that, while there is some room for efficiency, most standard users found the attack analyses fast enough. However, there are some situations where network enginerrs would need to analyse much larger networks, making speed more important.

### <img align="right" src="https://github.com/spe-uob/2023-NetworkTrafficAnalysis/assets/123552121/7260c8ff-47a4-40ad-a57a-0abda3e1207c"> Usefulness
When asked whether they found the attack analyses provided helpful, most testers agreed.

Testers thought the stats and explanations provided in the analysis were useful, but the ways in which they were presented could be improved by having clearer graphs and more consistent explanations.

### <img align="left" src="https://github.com/spe-uob/2023-NetworkTrafficAnalysis/assets/123552121/e95adbea-0ab6-4277-8de1-6881528877b9"> Understandability
After using the program, testers were asked if the attack analysis graphs were easy to understand. Responses were mixed.

Some users disagreed, suggesting that it was unclear what some of the graphs were telling them. Most testers said that they preferred the presentation of information in the new graphs over last year's graphs.
